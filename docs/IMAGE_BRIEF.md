# Image brief: Ramayana Storybook (v4)

Drop the files in `apps/v4-storybook/Packages/com.tableau.mcp.ramayana-storybook/content/img/` and send them to me. I will list them in `src/images.js` and republish.

## Format (all images)
- **Portrait 3:4, 1200 x 1600 px, JPG, under 400 KB each.** The left panel crops the sides on wide screens, so keep the subject in the centre 70%.
- **No text, letters or watermarks in the image.**
- **One consistent style** for every image. Suggested style line to add to every prompt:
  > *Indian miniature painting, Pahari/Kangra school, fine brushwork, warm palette of maroon, saffron, gold and deep green, soft parchment texture, reverent and calm, no text.*
- A Key Moment without its own image automatically uses its Kanda image, so the **8 required images come first**.
